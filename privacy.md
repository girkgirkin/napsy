
# Napsy Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 8, 2026

## What Napsy collects
If you choose to connect an Oura account, Napsy requests access to your sleep data through the Oura API, only with your permission. Napsy uses this data only to time your naps.

Napsy also saves your nap history, ratings and settings on your device.

## How your data is stored
Your nap history and settings are stored only on your device. Napsy does not run its own servers and does not upload your data anywhere. Oura data is used only while timing a nap and is not kept longer than needed.

## What Napsy does not do
- Napsy does not sell, rent or share your data with anyone.
- Napsy does not use your data for advertising or marketing.
- Napsy does not use your data to train or feed artificial intelligence.

## Your choices
- You can disconnect Napsy from your Oura account at any time in the Oura app or on the Oura website. Napsy will immediately stop accessing your Oura data.
- You can delete all Napsy data by deleting the app from your device.
- To request deletion of any data, contact us at the email below. Requests are handled within 72 hours.

## Changes to this policy
If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
